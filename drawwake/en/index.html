HOW IT WORKS

Three minutes,
and you're actually up.

⏰

It rings

Set the time, days, sound, snooze and time limit. Each alarm gets its own difficulty and prompt categories.

✏️

You draw

"Watermelon." "Parachute." Scribble it with your finger. Ask for up to five drawings in a row if one isn't enough to wake you.

☀️

You're awake

Finish drawing and the alarm stops. The drawing becomes a card, with your time and score recorded.

PROMPTS

Never the same morning.

Easy, food, animals, vehicles, nature, objects — pick the categories you like and get a prompt that suits the mood.

DRAWWAKE ONLY

Because alone is hard.

DrawWake turns "I'm up" into something that actually keeps you going.

One prompt for the whole group

Everyone in a group gets the same prompt for the day, so the drawings line up side by side. Your group streak is counted too.

Early-riser medals

🥇🥈🥉 for who got up first in the group. Not really a competition — but still nice.

Wake-time comparison

See your weekly average wake time next to your friends'. Harder to skip than a chart only you will look at.

The group's week

Export a single image summarising your group's week and share it.

GALLERY

The mornings
stack up.

Every drawing is saved automatically with its date and prompt. Six card styles, nine shadow colours, and a favourites filter for looking back.
